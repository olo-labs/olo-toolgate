# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want OLO ToolGate to be a technically rigorous and welcoming open-source project.

Expected behavior:

- discuss ideas and code, not people
- give actionable technical feedback
- assume good intent while still questioning designs
- welcome contributors of different experience levels
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or doxxing

## Scope

This applies to project repositories, Discussions, issues, pull requests, community spaces, and project events.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit/remove content, lock threads, restrict participation, or remove contributors when necessary to protect the community.

For sensitive conduct reports, contact project maintainers privately rather than posting personal details publicly.
